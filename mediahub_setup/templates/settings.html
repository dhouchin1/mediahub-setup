Caddy reverse proxy

Pick how the proxy exposes your services.

Local mode publishes each service on its own port and only accepts requests from RFC1918 + Tailscale ranges. Public mode routes everything under one hostname and requests a Let's Encrypt cert (needs ports 80/443 open).

For .local domains, add an entry to /etc/hosts pointing to 127.0.0.1.
